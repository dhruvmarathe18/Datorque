1. Introduction & App Description

Welcome to NIYANTRA ("the App"), a coaching institute management application developed and operated by Datorque ("we," "us," or "our"), a company based in India.

NIYANTRA is an Android mobile application designed to help coaching centers and educational institutes manage their day-to-day operations — including student enrollment, staff management, attendance tracking, fee collection records, homework assignments, test results, study material distribution, and internal communication.

The App supports four user roles:

  • Super Admin — Platform-level administrator
  • Coaching Admin — Institute-level administrator
  • Teacher / Staff — Instructors and support staff within an institute
  • Student — Enrolled learners (including minors managed by institute admins)

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, how we protect it, and what rights you have regarding your data. It applies to all users of the NIYANTRA app.

By using NIYANTRA, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the data necessary to provide the App's functionality. The data we collect varies by user role and is described below.

2.1 Student Information

  • First name, last name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, and gender
  • Parent/guardian name, parent/guardian phone number, and parent/guardian email address
  • Fee details: fee amount, fee type (monthly, quarterly, yearly, or one-time)

2.2 Staff / Teacher Information

  • First name, last name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, and gender
  • Designation, subjects taught, salary, and joining date

2.3 Admin Information

  • First name, last name, email address, and phone number

2.4 Institute Information

  • Institute name, contact person name, contact email, contact phone number, and address

2.5 Authentication Data

  • Email address and password — authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. Passwords are cryptographically hashed (bcrypt) and are never stored in plaintext.
  • Session tokens — stored locally on the user's device via AsyncStorage for maintaining login sessions.

2.6 Academic Data

  • Attendance records: date, status (present, absent, or late), and remarks
  • Test and exam results: marks obtained, grade, and percentage
  • Homework submissions: text content and file attachments
  • Study material: access history and engagement data

2.7 Financial Data

  • Fee amounts, due dates, and payment dates
  • Payment method (cash, UPI, card, bank transfer, or cheque) — recorded manually by admins only; the App does NOT process online payments
  • Transaction IDs and payment receipts

2.8 Communication Data

  • Chat messages: text content and file attachments (images, PDFs, documents)
  • Notice and announcement view tracking (which user viewed which notice)
  • Push notification tokens (Expo push tokens) for delivering notifications

2.9 Device Data

  • Device platform (Android/iOS)
  • Push notification token for notification delivery

2.10 User-Generated Content

  • Homework submission text and uploaded files
  • Chat messages and file attachments
  • Scanned question images — processed entirely on-device using Google ML Kit OCR. These images are NOT sent to any external server.

2.11 Data We Do NOT Collect

NIYANTRA does not collect any of the following:
  • GPS or location data
  • Contact lists or address books
  • Biometric data (fingerprint, face ID, etc.)
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking IDs
  • Browsing history
  • Social media account information
  • Call logs or SMS messages
  • Profile photos — avatars are auto-generated from user initials

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to provide, maintain, and improve the App's functionality. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Account management: Create and authenticate user accounts, maintain login sessions, and manage user roles and permissions within institutes.
  • Institute operations: Enable coaching admins to manage students, staff, batches, and institute-level settings.
  • Attendance tracking: Record and display daily attendance for students across batches.
  • Fee management: Track fee schedules, record manual payments, generate fee receipts, and send payment reminders.
  • Academic management: Manage homework assignments, test results, grades, and study materials.
  • Communication: Facilitate in-app chat between users, deliver notices and announcements, and send push notifications for important updates (attendance alerts, homework deadlines, fee reminders).
  • Reporting: Generate exportable reports (PDF/CSV) for attendance, fee records, and academic performance.
  • On-device text recognition: Use Google ML Kit to scan and extract text from question images — processed locally on the device without transmitting images to any server.
We do not use your personal data for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making, or any purpose unrelated to the App's core educational management functionality. We do not sell your data to any third party.

6. Data Storage & Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement the following measures:

6.1 Cloud Database

All user data is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies. RLS ensures that users can only access data belonging to their own institute — a teacher at Institute A cannot view any data from Institute B.

6.2 Cloud File Storage

User-uploaded files (homework submissions, chat attachments, study materials, and notice attachments) are stored on Cloudinary, a secure cloud-based media management platform.

6.3 Authentication Security

  • Passwords are cryptographically hashed using bcrypt via Supabase Auth and are never stored or transmitted in plaintext.
  • Authentication session tokens are stored locally on the user's device using AsyncStorage and are used solely to maintain active login sessions.

6.4 Data Isolation

Institute-level data is logically isolated through Row-Level Security policies. Each institute's data is accessible only to authorized users within that institute.

6.5 Data Transmission

All data transmitted between the App and our cloud services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption.

6.6 No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise commercialize your personal data to any third party, under any circumstances.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

7.1 Retention Period

User data is retained for as long as the associated account remains active within the App. Institute administrators control data retention within their respective institutes.

7.2 Account Deletion

When a student account is deleted by an institute administrator, all associated data is permanently removed, including:

  • Student profile and personal information
  • Authentication account and credentials
  • Batch and course enrollments
  • Attendance records
  • Fee payment records
  • Test and exam results
  • Homework submissions
  • Chat participation history
  • Push notification tokens

Similarly, when staff or teacher accounts are removed, their associated data is deleted.

7.3 Requesting Deletion

If you wish to have your data deleted, you may:

  • Request your institute administrator to delete your account through the App, or
  • Contact us directly at the email address provided in the Contact Us section below.

We will process deletion requests within 30 days of receipt.

8. Children's Privacy

NIYANTRA may be used by students under the age of 18 as part of their enrollment in coaching institutes. We are committed to protecting the privacy of minors and comply with applicable child data protection laws, including provisions under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023 of India and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) of the European Union.

Key safeguards for minors:

  • No direct sign-up by children: Student accounts are created and managed exclusively by coaching institute administrators — children do not create their own accounts.
  • Parental information: For minor students, the App collects parent/guardian name, phone number, and email address to ensure a responsible adult is associated with the student's account.
  • Limited data collection: We collect only the data necessary for educational management purposes. We do not collect data for advertising or profiling of minors.
  • Institute responsibility: Coaching institutes that use NIYANTRA are responsible for obtaining any required parental or guardian consent before enrolling minor students on the platform.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's data has been collected without proper consent, please contact us immediately at the email address in the Contact Us section, and we will take prompt action to address the issue.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

9.1 Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023

  • Right to access: You have the right to obtain a summary of your personal data and the processing activities related to it.
  • Right to correction: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or misleading personal data, and to have incomplete data completed.
  • Right to erasure: You have the right to request deletion of your personal data, subject to applicable legal obligations.
  • Right to grievance redressal: You have the right to register a complaint regarding the processing of your personal data.
  • Right to nominate: You have the right to nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

9.2 Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you additionally have the following rights:

  • Right to access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request deletion of your personal data under certain circumstances.
  • Right to restriction of processing: You may request that we limit the processing of your data in certain situations.
  • Right to data portability: You may request your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format. The App supports export of attendance records and reports in PDF and CSV formats.
  • Right to object: You may object to the processing of your personal data where we rely on legitimate interests as the legal basis.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

9.3 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of the above rights, you may:

  • Access, update, or export your data directly through the App (where the feature is available).
  • Request your institute administrator to make changes or delete your account.
  • Contact us directly at the email address provided in the Contact Us section.

We will respond to all rights-related requests within 30 days. If you are unsatisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
